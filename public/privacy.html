Pass Line — Privacy Policy

Effective 30 August 2026 · Contact: emoa2l@gmail.com

The short version

Pass Line collects nothing. The developer operates no server, and no data about you or your play ever reaches us.

What the app stores, and where

Your game history, saved sessions, and settings are stored only on your device. If you add an API key for an AI provider, it is stored in your device's secure keystore (iOS Keychain / Android encrypted preferences) and never leaves your device except to be sent to that provider.

The optional AI features

The coach and dealer are optional and off until you configure them. When you use them, the app sends your current table state — your bets, the point, and your chip counts — directly to the AI provider you chose, authenticated with your key, and only after you have agreed to an in-app notice. That data is handled under your provider's own privacy policy. We are not a party to it and receive nothing.

No name, email, location, contacts, photos, or device identifiers are ever sent to anyone. The app contains no analytics and no tracking of any kind.

Turning it off

Switch AI off in the app, or use "Forget key" to erase the credential. With AI off, the app makes no network connections at all — every other feature works entirely offline.

Purchases

The one-time Advanced unlock is processed by Apple's App Store or Google Play. We receive no payment details. Purchase state is verified through the store and cached on your device.

Children and real money

Pass Line is a play-money practice table. There is no real wagering, no prizes, and no way to buy chips. It includes simulated gambling and carries the age rating both stores assign for that.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted at this address with a new effective date.
